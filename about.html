About SOBEL

Social Organization for Better Living (SOBEL) is committed to improving children’s lives through education, advocacy, protection and welfare. Since 2022, we have worked with schools and communities to reach children who need support.

What we believe

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, feel safe and reach their full potential, regardless of their background.

Our vision

A world where every child can learn, grow, feel safe and reach their full potential.

Our mission

To improve children’s lives through education, advocacy, protection and welfare.

Our values

  • Child-centred
  • Integrity
  • Inclusion
  • Safety and protection
  • Collaboration
