What we do

Four areas of work, one aim: a better life for every child.

    • In schools
    • Learning environment

    Education

    We run outreach programmes in schools that teach, inspire and support students, and we work to improve their learning environment.

    • Campaigns
    • Talks
    • Social media

    Advocacy

    We raise awareness of children’s rights through campaigns, talks and social media, including our World Children’s Day campaign.

    • Safeguarding
    • Safety first

    Protection

    We promote child safeguarding and protection, and we run our programmes with the safety of children as the first priority.

    • Needs assessments
    • Practical support

    Welfare

    We identify the needs of schools and communities through needs assessments, and we respond with practical support.

Learning starts with the basics

Notebooks, pencils and desks help children take part in class. We bring them to the schools we visit.

How we work

  1. We assess needs.

  2. We plan and budget.

  3. We deliver the outreach with schools and volunteers.

  4. We report on what we did and what we learned.
